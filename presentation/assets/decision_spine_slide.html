Anomaly review

What
Account transactions, in a batch.
Why
Unusual patterns surface as complaints, not alerts. By then the money has moved.

Reporting assistance

What
The quarter's complaint figures.
Why
Built by hand from spreadsheets, five days a quarter, and every figure has to be right.

AI

The AI reads the complaint and picks the team from a fixed list — and must quote the customer's own sentence as its reason.

Rules, then AI

Rules pick the case out of the ledger. The AI never decides what is suspicious — it only explains the case the rules raised.

Computed, then AI

The figures are calculated first. The AI only writes the narrative around numbers it did not produce.
